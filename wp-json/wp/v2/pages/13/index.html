Dean Powell is an English artist interested in self-overcoming by means of spatial audio, field recording and sound installation.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

His work is underpinned by a reverence for rare moments of fleeting beauty that are bound up within the mundane, accessible by means of an embodied contemplative listening that transcends superficialities and prompts a revaluation of values concerning the acquisition of knowledge.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Employing a range of techniques that span walking, coding, binaural acousmatic film and the repurposing of everyday objects, he examines the tensions between logocentrism and the intuitive apprehension of truth. Themes of impermanence, the passage of duration and the power of repetition permeate his works, which aim at loosening the seductive domestication of words and kindling a spark of curiosity in the listener with regards to the supra-mundane potentialities of human consciousness.<\/p>\n","protected":false},"excerpt":{"rendered":"
