Dean Powell is an English artist whose work illuminates the dynamic between contemplation, change and self-overcoming.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

These efforts are underpinned by a reverence for rare moments of fleeting beauty bound up within the everyday, and accessible by means of an attentive practice of contemplative listening.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Through a variety of techniques that span walking, coding, film and the repurposing of everyday objects, his work invites audiences to actively engage with the mundane as a way of cultivating a sonic intuition of the supra-mundane.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
