Dean Powell is an English artist whose work attempts to illuminate the dynamic between contemplation, metamorphosis and self-overcoming.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

These efforts are underpinned by a reverence for rare moments of fleeting beauty bound up within the mundane, refractory to law or measurement, yet accessible by means of an embodied practice of contemplative listening\u2014one that transcends superficialities of label and form\u2014thereby prompting a revaluation of values concerning, for example, the acquisition of knowledge.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Through a variety of techniques that span walking, coding, binaural acousmatic film and the repurposing of everyday objects, he examines the tensions between logocentrism and the intuitive apprehension of truth. Themes of impermanence, the passage of duration and the power of repetition permeate his works, which aim at loosening the seductive domestication of words and kindling a spark of curiosity in the listener with regards to supra-mundane potentialities of human consciousness.<\/p>\n","protected":false},"excerpt":{"rendered":"
